Dota 2 Party Maker Bot
A Discord bot designed for creating "LFG" (Looking For Group) announcements for Dota 2.

Features:
Mode Selection: Choose between Ranked, Normal, Battlecup, Inhouse and Low prio.

Automatic Thread Creation: Generates a dedicated chat thread for every group, attached to its announcement.

Auto-Cleanup: Announcements automatically warn after 25 minutes and expire after 30 minutes of inactivity (reset by clicking "Przedłuż" or by staying in the selected voice channel).

Thread Management: Automatically removes threads 24 hours after their announcement closes.
